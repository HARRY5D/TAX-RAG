# Virtual Digital Assets (VDA) / Cryptocurrency — Tax Guide FY 2025-26

## What is a Virtual Digital Asset (VDA)?
Under Section 2(47A) of the Income Tax Act, a VDA includes:
- Cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, etc.)
- Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs)
- Any digital asset defined by the Central Government

---

## CRITICAL: Section 44ADA Does NOT Apply to Crypto

**WRONG (do not say this):** Crypto profits can be reported under Section 44ADA presumptive taxation at 50% of gross receipts.

**CORRECT:** Section 44ADA applies ONLY to professionals earning from professional services. Cryptocurrency trading/transfer gains are governed EXCLUSIVELY by Section 115BBH. The two sections are completely separate and cannot be mixed.

**WRONG (do not say this):** Section 87A rebate reduces crypto tax to zero.

**CORRECT:** Section 87A rebate applies only to slab-taxed income under Section 115BAC. VDA income taxed under Section 115BBH is at a special flat rate. The 87A rebate CANNOT be applied to Section 115BBH income. You will NOT get zero tax on crypto gains regardless of your total income level.

---

## Section 115BBH — Flat Tax on VDA Transfers

### Tax Rate
- **30% flat tax** on income from transfer of any Virtual Digital Asset
- **Plus 4% Health and Education Cess** on the base tax
- **Effective tax rate = 31.2%** (30% + 4% of 30%)

### What Counts as a "Transfer"?
- Selling crypto for cash/INR
- Swapping one crypto for another (e.g., Bitcoin to Ethereum)
- Using crypto to purchase goods/services
- Receiving crypto as payment for work

### Allowed Deduction
- **ONLY the cost of acquisition** of the specific VDA is deductible
- NO deduction for:
  - Trading fees or exchange commissions
  - Gas fees / network fees
  - Internet or electricity costs
  - Any other business expenses
  - Any Chapter VI-A deduction (80C, 80D, NPS, etc.)

---

## Section 194S — TDS on Crypto Transactions

| Condition | TDS Rate |
|---|---|
| Transaction by any person | 1% of sale consideration |
| Threshold for non-specified persons | Rs.10,000 per transaction |
| Threshold for specified persons | Rs.50,000 per year |

**Key points:**
- TDS is deducted at 1% of the total sale value (not just the gain)
- TDS deducted under Section 194S can be claimed as a tax credit when filing ITR
- The buyer (or crypto exchange) is responsible for deducting TDS

---

## No Loss Set-Off Between VDAs

This is a critical rule under Section 115BBH(2):

- Loss from one VDA CANNOT be set off against gain from another VDA
  - Example: Bitcoin loss of Rs.5 lakh + Ethereum gain of Rs.8 lakh means you must pay 30% tax on Rs.8 lakh gain. The Rs.5 lakh Bitcoin loss gives NO benefit.
- VDA losses CANNOT be set off against any other income (salary, business, rent, etc.)
- VDA losses CANNOT be carried forward to future years
- Each VDA transaction is treated in isolation

## Step-by-Step Tax Calculation

### Formula
Gross Consideration (sale price)
minus Cost of Acquisition (purchase price only)
= Net VDA Income (taxable)

Tax = 30% x Net VDA Income
Cess = 4% x Tax
Total Tax = Tax + Cess (= 31.2% of Net VDA Income)

TDS already deducted (194S) = 1% x Gross Consideration
Net payable at ITR = Total Tax - TDS Credit

### Worked Example 1: Basic Profit
| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Purchase price (cost of acquisition) | Rs.12,00,000 |
| Sale price (gross consideration) | Rs.20,00,000 |
| Net VDA Income (taxable) | Rs.8,00,000 |
| Base Tax at 30% | Rs.2,40,000 |
| Health and Education Cess at 4% | Rs.9,600 |
| Total Tax = 31.2% of Rs.8L | Rs.2,49,600 |
| TDS deducted under Section 194S (1% of Rs.20L) | Rs.20,000 |
| Net cash payable during ITR filing | Rs.2,29,600 |

Note: Section 87A rebate does NOT apply here. Tax is Rs.2,49,600, not zero.

### Worked Example 2: Multiple Coins (No Cross Set-Off)
| Asset | Buy Price | Sell Price | Gain/Loss | Tax |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bitcoin | Rs.5,00,000 | Rs.9,00,000 | +Rs.4,00,000 | Rs.1,24,800 at 31.2% |
| Ethereum | Rs.3,00,000 | Rs.1,50,000 | -Rs.1,50,000 | Rs.0 (loss, no benefit) |
| Total | | | | Rs.1,24,800 |

Bitcoin gain cannot be reduced by Ethereum loss. Pay full tax on Bitcoin profit.

## Regime Comparison for Crypto

| Item | Old Tax Regime | New Tax Regime |
|---|---|---|
| VDA Tax Rate | 31.2% flat (Section 115BBH) | 31.2% flat (Section 115BBH) |
| Section 87A Rebate | Does NOT apply | Does NOT apply |
| Chapter VI-A deductions (80C etc.) | Not allowed for VDA | Not allowed for VDA |
| Regime choice affects crypto tax? | No - crypto is always 31.2% | No - crypto is always 31.2% |

Key insight: Your choice of Old vs New Tax Regime has ZERO effect on crypto tax. It is always 31.2%.

## Common Mistakes (DO NOT REPEAT THESE)

1. WRONG: Crypto profits under Rs.12 lakh are tax-free due to 87A rebate.
   CORRECT: 87A rebate does NOT apply to Section 115BBH VDA income. Every rupee of crypto gain is taxed at 31.2%.

2. WRONG: Report crypto income under Section 44ADA at 50% of gross.
   CORRECT: Section 44ADA is for professionals only. Crypto is taxed under Section 115BBH, not 44ADA.

3. WRONG: You can deduct exchange fees and gas fees from crypto gains.
   CORRECT: Only cost of acquisition is deductible. No fees, commissions, or expenses are allowed.

4. WRONG: Losses from one crypto can offset gains from another.
   CORRECT: Section 115BBH(2) explicitly prohibits cross-VDA loss set-off.

5. WRONG: Crypto losses can be carried forward.
   CORRECT: VDA losses cannot be carried forward under any provision.

---

## Filing / ITR Requirement

- Report VDA gains in Schedule VDA in ITR-2 or ITR-3
- If you have only salary + VDA income: use ITR-2
- If you have business income + VDA income: use ITR-3
- Claim TDS credit (Section 194S) in Schedule TDS
