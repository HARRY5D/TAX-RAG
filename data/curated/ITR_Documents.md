# ITR Documents — Form 16, Form 26AS, AIS, TIS Guide FY 2025-26

## Form 16 — TDS Certificate from Employer

Form 16 is issued by the employer to every salaried employee by 15 June after the end of the financial year.
It is the primary document for filing ITR for salaried individuals.

### Part A — TDS Summary
- Employer name, address, TAN, PAN
- Employee name and PAN
- Quarter-wise summary of TDS deducted and deposited with government
- Acknowledgement number of TDS deposited

### Part B — Salary and Deduction Details
- Gross salary: basic pay, HRA, LTA, special allowance, perquisites
- Exemptions claimed: HRA exemption under Section 10(13A), LTA under Section 10(5)
- Standard deduction: Rs.50,000 (Old Regime) or Rs.75,000 (New Regime)
- Income chargeable under "Salaries"
- Deductions under Chapter VI-A: 80C, 80D, 80CCD(1B), 80TTA etc (Old Regime only)
- Net taxable income
- Tax payable / refundable
- TDS deducted from salary

Key action: Verify that your Form 16 Part A TDS figures match Form 26AS exactly.
Any mismatch can cause ITR processing delays or tax notices.

## Form 26AS — Tax Credit Statement

Form 26AS is maintained by the Income Tax Department and linked to your PAN.
It shows ALL tax deducted/collected at source from ALL deductors, not just your employer.

### What is in Form 26AS?
- Part A: TDS deducted on salary (by employer — Section 192)
- Part A1: TDS deducted on other income (by banks on FD interest — Section 194A)
- Part B: TCS (Tax Collected at Source) by sellers
- Part C: Advance tax and self-assessment tax paid directly
- Part D: Refunds received from Income Tax Dept
- Part F: TDS on rent, professional fees, etc.

### Why Form 26AS is Critical
- It is the official record of tax credit against your PAN
- Use it to verify TDS from employer, bank, and any other deductor
- If a bank deducted TDS on FD interest, it will appear in Part A1
- Any discrepancy between Form 16 and Form 26AS must be resolved before filing

How to download: Income Tax portal (incometax.gov.in) -> e-File -> Income Tax Returns -> View Form 26AS

## Annual Information Statement (AIS)

AIS is a comprehensive 360-degree financial profile linked to your PAN.
It is more detailed than Form 26AS and includes all reported transactions.

### What AIS Shows
- Salary income reported by employer
- Interest income from savings accounts (banks report to IT dept)
- Interest income from FDs, RDs, post office deposits
- Dividend income from shares and mutual funds
- Sale/purchase of securities (shares, mutual funds)
- Sale/purchase of immovable property
- Foreign remittances received or sent
- GST turnover reported (for businesses)
- Cash deposits/withdrawals above Rs.10 lakh
- Credit card spends above Rs.10 lakh

### Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS)
TIS is a consolidated, category-wise summary of AIS data.
It shows "Processed value" (agreed) and "Derived value" (IT department's calculation).

### Key Actions
1. Download AIS from IT portal: e-File -> AIS
2. Check every entry — report errors using "Feedback" option if data is wrong
3. Cross-check AIS interest income with your actual bank statements
4. All income shown in AIS must be reported in ITR (even if you think it is exempt)

## Complete Document Checklist for Salaried ITR Filing

| Document | Purpose | Where to Get |
|---|---|---|
| Form 16 (Part A + B) | Salary TDS certificate | Employer HR/Finance |
| Form 26AS | Tax credit verification | IT portal (incometax.gov.in) |
| AIS / TIS | All income and transactions | IT portal -> e-File -> AIS |
| Bank statements (all accounts) | FD interest, savings interest | Bank netbanking |
| Form 16A | TDS on FD interest by bank | Bank netbanking / branch |
| Home loan interest certificate | Section 24(b) deduction | Bank / NBFC |
| PPF passbook / ELSS statement | Section 80C proof | Bank / AMC |
| Health insurance premium receipt | Section 80D proof | Insurance company |
| Rent receipts + landlord PAN | HRA exemption proof | Landlord |
| NPS account statement | Section 80CCD(1B) proof | NPS Trust / NSDL |
| Capital gain statements | Report stock/MF sales | Broker / AMC |

## Step-by-Step ITR Filing Process

1. Download Form 26AS and AIS from IT portal
2. Compare with Form 16 — resolve any discrepancies
3. Check all interest income in AIS against bank statements
4. Go to IT portal -> e-File -> File Income Tax Return
5. Select Assessment Year (AY 2026-27 for FY 2025-26)
6. Use "Pre-fill" — portal auto-populates salary, TDS, and some income from Form 26AS and AIS
7. Verify pre-filled data carefully — do not blindly accept
8. Add any income NOT pre-filled (FD interest from small banks, savings interest, etc.)
9. Claim deductions (Old Regime: 80C, 80D, 80TTA etc)
10. Verify total tax calculated, compare with TDS deducted
11. Pay any remaining tax (self-assessment tax) before filing
12. Submit and e-verify using Aadhaar OTP, net banking, or DSC

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. NOT reporting FD interest (banks report to IT dept via AIS — they will catch it)
2. Forgetting savings interest income (even Rs.5,000 must be declared)
3. Not claiming TDS credit from Form 26AS (leads to paying double tax)
4. Using wrong ITR form (ITR-1 when ITR-2 is needed due to capital gains)
5. Not verifying AIS before filing (AIS may have errors that could inflate tax)
6. Missing the 31 July deadline (attracts late fee of Rs.5,000 under Section 234F)
